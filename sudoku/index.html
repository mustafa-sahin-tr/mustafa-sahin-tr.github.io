Classic sudoku, or race the countdown

Play relaxed Classic with no clock pressure, or Time Race, where you solve before the countdown hits zero. Every day there are four Daily Puzzles and one Daily Race: the same puzzle for everyone, with points for speed and a world ranking.

Support

Questions, problems or ideas: write to mtafasahin@gmail.com. Please include your phone model and the app version (Settings).

Purchases: Settings › Restore purchases brings Remove Ads back on a new phone. Progress: link Apple or Google Play Games in Settings › Account to keep your progress on a new phone.

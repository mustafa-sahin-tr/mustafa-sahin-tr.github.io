EnglishTürkçe

Tap a piece, the board tilts, everything slides

Every piece has a weight. Tap one and it is gone; the heavier side of the board sinks, pieces slide toward it, and the ones that pass an open edge drop into the basket. The weight changes again, and a single tap can set off a chain. Clear the board without dropping the gold. No timer, no lives: just a tap budget.
